libmpv (Windows x64, LGPL build) — Build Configuration & Source Attribution
===========================================================================

OpenTV v2 optionally bundles `bin/mpv-2.dll` (loaded dynamically via
`libloading`, never statically linked) for hardware-accelerated playback on
Windows. The user may replace `bin/mpv-2.dll` with any ABI-compatible
`libmpv-2.dll` build.
